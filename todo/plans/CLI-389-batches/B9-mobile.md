## Dispositions — category placement and exclusivity

**A. `mobile-framework` stays `exclusive: false` — CONFIRMED, nothing to do.** The worksheet
asked for verification and it checks out end to end: the category is open + `required: true` in
the matrix, `expo requires [react-native]` exists at default-rules.ts:493-497 (so expo+react-native
is not just permitted but structurally expected), and the paired `compatibleWith` group at
281-284 has its parity `requires` per decision 4a — it converts to nothing and deletes clean.
This is the one place in the catalog where two same-category "framework" skills legitimately
co-select; flipping it exclusive would make every Expo stack unrepresentable.

**B. `mobile-navigation` radio — CORRECT, keep.** Expo Router is built on top of React
Navigation, but as _skills_ they are competing navigation paradigms: file-based routes owned by
the Expo SDK versus explicitly declared navigators. An app has one router. The subsumption
relationship makes co-selection incoherent rather than merely unusual — the radio states a real
pick-one fact that `requires` cannot (both members' requires are satisfiable simultaneously in
an Expo stack, so without the radio nothing fences them).

**C. `mobile-styling` radio — CORRECT, keep.** One styling system per app is the same
pick-one semantics as the planned `web-ui-kit`/`shared-lint` splits (§2b). NativeWind and
Unistyles both patch RN's styling pipeline at build/runtime level; running both is technically
possible and practically pathological. Both members' requires are co-satisfiable, so the radio
is load-bearing, not redundant.

**D. `mobile-testing` radio — DROP: flip to `exclusive: false` (verification item 3 overturned
the keep).** Detox (gray-box, instrumented) and Maestro (black-box, UI-layer) are different
_kinds_ of testing tool with a documented complementary steady-state pattern — Maestro for
broad cross-platform smoke flows, Detox for gray-box depth (maestro.dev's own Detox comparison
content; the 2026 comparison round-ups) — a standing pattern, not just a migration window. The
original consistency defense fails on kind: playwright/cypress (worksheet #6's exclusive
`web-e2e`) are same-kind substitutes, while the catalog's own precedent for different-kind
testing tools is the OPEN `web-testing` residue (vitest + playwright compose). Both members'
`requires` bindings still fence them into mobile stacks; the flip is pure loosening, so no
fenceless window.

**E. `mobile-ui-components` radio — CORRECT, keep.** One component kit per app — identical
pick-one semantics to the `web-ui-kit` split of group #14. Tamagui vs RN Paper is a genuine
either/or design-system choice. Tamagui's widened `needsAny` (react added, verify item 2) and
its D-306 curation note do not change the within-mobile radio (verify item 8).

**F. The ten open categories — no changes.** animation (3 members) and storage (3) genuinely
compose: reanimated + gesture-handler + skia is the _canonical_ modern RN animation stack, and
mmkv (KV) + powersync or watermelondb (offline DB) coexist by design. The seven single-member
categories (deployment, camera, hardware, notifications, background, deep-linking, performance,
security) are closed completely by their `requires` binding; open + single-member needs no
radio. No moves, no merges, no new categories.

**G. Apply-phase ordering constraint.** The 22 `requires` rules are purely additive, and the
batch's one radio flip — `mobile-testing` to `exclusive: false` (disposition D) — is a pure
loosening, so there is still no fenceless window (unlike B10's un-flags); land the flip in the
same change as the detox/maestro bindings. The only sequencing hazard is the known EDITOR-11
one: the CLI computes no requires-closure, so the multi-hop chains this batch creates
(expo-router → expo → react-native → react) must not become verdict-load-bearing before the
shared closure in `packages/matrix` lands. The flat `needsAny` lists are written to be correct
even without closure — that is why react-native appears beside expo everywhere.
