## Doctrine amendments the hard cases force

**The separate-deployable rule (settles item 1, the taught-surface inversion).** Taught-surface
derivation asks what a skill's patterns presume about the _project's app stack_ — and the
followability test must run against the skill's actual host. When framework-flavored content
executes inside a self-contained deployable the skill itself installs (a docs site, an admin
studio), the framework is internal to that deployable — a package fact at one remove — and
derives **nothing**, even when the content is genuinely taught rather than merely present
(VitePress's Vue-in-Markdown is real teaching and still derives no fence: a VitePress theme is
Vue no matter what the app is, and `vitepress` vendors its own Vue). Precedents now forming one
line: sanity Studio, payload admin, and the doc tools. Distinguish nextauth/setup-axiom: their
content executes in the project's own app tree — same deployable — so taught surface binds. The
axis is never "is the content framework-flavored" but "which deployable runs it". CLI-740's
content-flavor note (B1's proposal) is the right residue vocabulary for what the dropped fence
leaves behind.

**The as-catalogued fence discriminator (items 3 vs 15, stated once).** A hard fence between
co-satisfiable skills is sustained _as catalogued_ only when co-selection yields contradictory
unconditional MUSTs (biome/eslint-prettier, zustand/jotai). When the pair's own texts route users
across the boundary (react-query's When-NOT → tRPC; upstream's re-use-the-QueryClient), the fence
is the bullmq/upstash shape and falls under the enforcement asymmetry. Alternatives-layer purpose
groups are advisory and never sustain a fence on their own — membership in "Server State / Data
Fetching" is what CLI-740 records, not what a radio enforces.

**The bridge-extension bar (item 15).** An official interop extension between two same-kind tools
(jotai-zustand) does not by itself reach the vendor-coexistence bar that overturned the
mobile-testing radio: bridging one tool's state INTO the other's consumption model is not two
peers doing different jobs. It does, however, obligate the record — such extensions must be cited
in the CLI-740 line and named in the reopen trigger, never waved off as nonexistent.
